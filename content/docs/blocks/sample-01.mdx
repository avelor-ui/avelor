## What flips in RTL

- The section mirrors as a whole: text aligns to the start edge, the stat column moves to the opposite side and its accent border sits on the start side (`border-s-2`, `ps-6`).
- The arrow icon in the call to action is marked `data-rtl="flip"` and mirrors with `rtl:-scale-x-100`.
- Numbers are formatted with `Intl.NumberFormat` for the selected locale; Arabic keeps Western digits.

## Reduced motion behaviour

The entrance reveal runs once when the section scrolls into view. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, or with the Motion control set to Reduced, the final state renders immediately with no transition. Without JavaScript the content is fully visible through a `noscript` style.
